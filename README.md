## 🌟 What is CAREEROS AI?

**CAREEROS AI** is an AI-powered **Career Professional & Development Operating System** designed to guide a person through the complete career journey — not just answer career-related questions.

Instead of simply telling users:

> ❌ "What is an AI Engineer?"

CAREEROS AI asks:

> **What is the profession?**  
> **What does the professional actually do?**  
> **What skills are required?**  
> **Where am I currently?**  
> **What skills am I missing?**  
> **What should I learn next?**  
> **What should I build?**  
> **How can I prove my skills?**  
> **How should I present myself professionally?**  
> **How should I prepare my resume?**  
> **How can I practice interviews?**  
> **Am I ready to apply?**  
> **How can I continue growing after getting a job?**
